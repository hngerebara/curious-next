school Teacher & Diagnostic Notes

Attackers can freely copy logos, color palettes, and header styling from any legitimate organization. A familiar logo proves only that an image was copied, not that the sender is authentic. Always verify via an independent trusted route (such as opening the app directly or typing the known web address yourself).
