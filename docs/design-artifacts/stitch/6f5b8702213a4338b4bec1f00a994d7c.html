How It Works

Structured guidance built to respect adult reasoning and practical demands.

1

Set practical goal & explanation tone

Define the problem you want to solve and choose how technical you want the foundational vocabulary to be.

2

Match course & optional baseline

Select your module track. Optionally complete an introductory review so concepts meet your exact existing knowledge.

3

Learn, Practise, and Teach-back

Go through clear explanations, solve contextual scenario exercises, and articulate the rationale in your own words.

4

Criterion-referenced feedback

Receive evaluation tied to concrete criteria, identifying what is demonstrated, developing, or requiring another look.
