AI-2 · Learn to write clear prompts

flag
Learning Objective

Write a prompt with task, context, constraints, and format, then check the response.

Large language models are pattern-matching engines rather than conscious problem solvers. Without precise guardrails, the model fills in blanks with plausible-sounding fabrications. Writing clear prompts is simply the discipline of drawing boundaries around what the model is allowed to generate.

1. Brief: Define the practical problem without prompting the LLM yet. Pin down the audience and raw information source.

2. Prompt: Construct the four structural boundaries: Task, Context, Constraints, and Format.

3. Response: Allow the model to generate candidates strictly within the established bounds.

4. Check: Audit the output sentence by sentence against source facts and length criteria.

Worked Example: River Cafe FAQ

Annotated

“River Cafe facts: Opens Mon–Sat 08:00–17:00. Vegetarian lunch daily. Walk-ins only; no table bookings. No delivery or takeaway services.”
The 4-Part Prompt Anatomy Copy-ready
1
Task
“Draft 4 concise customer FAQ entries.”
2
Context
“You are an information desk assistant for River Cafe, a local daytime eatery.”
3
Constraints
“Rely exclusively on the supplied cafe facts above. Do not invent evening hours, reservation links, or menu items.”
4
Format
“Four bulleted Q&As, each answer strictly under 35 words.”

    Q: Do you offer vegetarian food?

    Q: Do you offer takeaway or home delivery?
