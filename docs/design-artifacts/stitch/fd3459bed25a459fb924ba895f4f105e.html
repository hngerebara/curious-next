lightbulb Observation Hint 1

Notice the difference in urgency and request type between the two communications.

Look closely at phrasing like "immediate" or "destroyed" versus neutral scheduling reminders.
search_insights Action Hint 2

Check what each message actually asks you to do: does one demand payment on an external link?

Authentic service providers do not threaten instant property destruction over small nominal fees.
Reflection question CY-2 Concept

"What would you do if you actually WERE awaiting a courier package?"
