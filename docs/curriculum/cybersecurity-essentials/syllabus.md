# Cybersecurity Essentials syllabus

## Review status

Draft, pending subject review. Publication is gated by the [reference register](../../references/curriculum-sources.md).

## Assessment baseline

Each module has one objective ID, assessed through distinct practical and conceptual criteria. All criteria listed below are required for initial demonstration. Hints make the attempt assisted. Successful supported practice establishes Developing, not Demonstrated. Recall checks retention and never substitutes for initial practical proof. Transfer uses a new context with the same objective.


## Modules


Outcome: recognise common threats and make justified protective decisions in simulations. This is introductory personal digital security, not a professional cybersecurity qualification.

| Module / objective | Prerequisite | Learn and practise | Independent application and teach-back criteria | Remediation | Delayed check / transfer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CY-1: Understand digital risk | None | Map assets, threats, and likely consequences in a fictional routine. | Identify assets and plausible threats, prioritise protective actions with reasons, and distinguish likelihood from certainty. | Contrast a threat with its consequence. | Revisit a changed routine. Transfer from a personal to a small-business scenario. |
| CY-2: Recognise phishing | CY-1 readiness | Inspect messages and separate suspicious cues from proof. | Identify supported cues, avoid unsafe interaction, choose independent verification/reporting, and explain why appearance does not establish authenticity. | Contrast a familiar logo with independently verified sender identity. | Later evaluate a fresh message. Transfer from delivery to account-security messages. |
| CY-3: Protect accounts | CY-1 readiness | Simulated unique-password, password-manager, MFA/passkey, and recovery choices. | Choose appropriate available protections, avoid credential sharing/reuse, plan recovery, and explain remaining risks. | Repair one unsafe setting and explain its consequence. | Recall recovery decisions later. Transfer to another fictional service. |
| CY-4: Protect devices and data | CY-1, CY-3 readiness | Updates, permissions, sharing, and backups in a simulation. | Apply necessary updates, choose limited permissions/sharing, plan backups, and explain tradeoffs without unsupported guarantees. | Rework the specific excessive permission or missing backup. | Review a changed setting later. Transfer to a different device context. |
| CY-5: Respond to compromise | CY-2, CY-3, CY-4 readiness | Guided response branches for clicked links, entered credentials, or installed software. | Distinguish incident facts, choose containment and trusted recovery actions suited to the branch, report appropriately, and justify order. | Revisit the unsafe or missing response step. | Later explain the first response steps. Transfer to a new incident branch. |

The same readiness rules apply. Do not demand identical incident sequences when multiple sequences are safe. Scenario answer keys specify acceptable branches and disallowed actions. No working phishing links, real credentials, or real account changes are used.
