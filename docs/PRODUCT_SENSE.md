# CuriousNext product principles

## Decisions

Goals lead to course choice. Evidence guides the pathway. Learners retain control over starting point and explanation preferences. Personalisation changes support and context, not standards. Accessibility is a baseline. Language choice does not imply ability.

## Evaluation

Evaluate independent application, reasoning, retention and transfer separately. Course completion, activity counts and positive feedback alone do not demonstrate learning gains. State pilot and assessment limits honestly. Keep opportunity guidance grounded in portable evidence rather than employment promises.
