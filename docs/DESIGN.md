# CuriousNext design guidance

## Experience principles

One primary next action, with up to two alternatives. Show the goal, relevant objective, estimate and evidence-based reason. Preserve context between lesson, practice and feedback. Progress communicates ability and review needs rather than a combined score.

## Accessibility and language

Use equivalent structured text for visuals, labelled controls, visible focus and readable reflow. Never rely on colour alone. Language and explanation level are independent. Approved language variants preserve learning and assessment standards.

Visual tokens and component choices are pending implementation decisions, not an existing design system. [Pilot specification](product-specs/pilot-design.md), [frontend](FRONTEND.md).
